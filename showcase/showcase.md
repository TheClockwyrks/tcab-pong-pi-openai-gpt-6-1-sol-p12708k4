# Carom

Not just back and forth. Carom is a paddle duel where a moving strike bends the ball through a dark, luminous court. A clean center hit buys you control; a sweeping edge hit asks your opponent a much harder question.

The first serve gives you a moment to find your position. Then the rally accelerates with every paddle return. Chase the ball, meet it on the move, and watch its trail curl away from the line your opponent expected.

Two golden obstacles sway and rotate between you. Their tilted faces can rescue a missed angle or send a confident return straight back at you. Learn to read the bank, slip through the opening, or turn the obstacle into part of your shot.

Take on the machine in Solo, or share the keyboard for a local Versus match. First to eleven takes it, but a tied match demands a two-point lead. At match point, one well-timed curve can be the difference between another rally and the winning shot.
